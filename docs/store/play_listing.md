## Full description (1912/4000)
Your ball is the brush and the maze is your canvas! Maze Brush: Paint Puzzle Game is a relaxing maze game where one swipe sends the ball sliding until it hits a wall, painting every tile it rolls over. Paint the whole maze to win.

Easy to learn, hard to master. The first levels teach you the moves; then the mazes grow, the routes get trickier and every level has a move limit that rewards smart planning.

🎨 HOW TO PLAY
• Swipe up, down, left or right to slide the ball
• The ball keeps rolling until it reaches a wall
• Paint every tile in the maze to complete the level
• Finish in the fewest moves to earn 3 stars

🧩 5000 MAZE PUZZLES
• 5000 levels, from calm warm-ups to real brain teasers
• Every level is checked to be solvable, so you never get truly stuck
• New tiles keep it fresh: stoppers, one-way gates, deflectors and portals
• Free undo and restart, plus hints when you need a nudge

📅 DAILY CHALLENGE
• A brand-new maze puzzle every day
• Keep your streak going and earn bonus coins
• Collect daily rewards on a 7-day calendar

🌍 10 BEAUTIFUL WORLDS
Paint your way through Golden Teak, Marble Hall, Pastel Clay, Walnut Study, Frost Ice, Zen Sand, Jade Stone, Slate Night, Cherry Wood and Sandstone. Every maze is carved into a real-looking surface with smooth, satisfying paint effects.

✨ COLLECT AND CUSTOMIZE
