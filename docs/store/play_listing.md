## Full description (1916/4000)
Swipe, slide and splash color across every tile! Splashy Glide is a relaxing maze puzzle game where one simple swipe sends your ball gliding until it hits a wall, painting every tile it rolls over. Paint the whole maze to win.

Easy to learn, hard to master. The first levels teach you the moves; then the mazes get bigger, the routes get trickier and every level has a move limit that rewards clever planning.

🎨 HOW TO PLAY
• Swipe up, down, left or right to slide the ball
• The ball keeps gliding until it reaches a wall
• Paint every tile in the maze to complete the level
• Finish in the fewest moves to earn 3 stars

🧩 5000 VERIFIED MAZE PUZZLES
• 5000 levels, from calm warm-ups to real brain teasers
• Every level is checked to be solvable, so you can never get truly stuck
• New tiles keep it fresh: stoppers, one-way gates, deflectors and portals
• Undo and restart for free, and use a hint when you need a nudge

📅 DAILY CHALLENGE

• Keep your streak going and earn bonus coins
• Claim daily rewards on a 7-day calendar

🌍 10 BEAUTIFUL WORLDS
Glide through Golden Teak, Marble Hall, Pastel Clay, Walnut Study, Frost Ice, Zen Sand, Jade Stone, Slate Night, Cherry Wood and Sandstone. Each maze is carved into a real-looking surface with smooth, satisfying paint effects.
