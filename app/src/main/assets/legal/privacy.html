Privacy Policy

Splashy Glide · Effective 18 November 2026

The short version

Splashy Glide ("the Game"), published by [Developer name], does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or photos. There is no account to create. Your game progress stays on your device.

What the Game stores on your device

  • Game progress: unlocked levels, stars, coins, hints, owned and selected skins.
  • Settings: sound, music and vibration on/off.
  • Daily features: the last day you played the Daily Challenge and claimed a daily reward.

This information is stored only in the Game's private storage on your device. We do not receive it. It is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its data. If your device's Android backup is on, Android may include it in your own device backup.

Permissions

The Game only uses the vibration permission, for optional haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings. It does not use the internet in this version.

Advertising (future versions)

Future versions may show ads provided by third-party ad networks. Those partners may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), app and ad-interaction data to show and measure ads. Before ads are added, this policy will be updated to list each partner, the data collected and how you can control personalised ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings at any time.

Children

The Game is suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your choices

  • Turn sound, music and vibration on or off in Settings.
  • Delete all Game data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the Game.
