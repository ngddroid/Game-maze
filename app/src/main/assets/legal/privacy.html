Privacy Policy

Maze Brush: Paint Puzzle Game · Effective 23 November 2026

The short version

Maze Brush: Paint Puzzle Game ("Maze Brush" or "the Game", Android package com.ngstudio.mazebrush), published by NGENDEV TECHNOLABS LLP (NG Studio), does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or photos. There is no account to create. Your game progress stays on your device. To fix crashes and improve the Game, it sends anonymous usage statistics and crash reports to Google Firebase (see below). Using the Game is also covered by our Terms of Use.

What the Game stores on your device

  • Game progress: unlocked levels, stars, coins, hints, owned and selected skins.
  • Settings: sound, music and vibration on/off.
  • Daily features: the last day you played the Daily Challenge and claimed a daily reward.

This information is stored only in the Game's private storage on your device. We do not receive it. It is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its data. If your device's Android backup is on, Android may include it in your own device backup.

Analytics and crash reports

The Game uses two services from Google LLC: Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. They collect:

  • How the Game is used: screens opened, levels started and finished, stars, moves, hints, coins earned and spent, and rewards claimed.
  • Crash reports: what went wrong in the app, the screen and level at the time, and the app version.
  • Device and app information: a random app-instance ID, device model, Android version, language, approximate country (from the network) and app version.

The Game does not collect the advertising ID, and ad personalisation is switched off.

This data does not include your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location, and we do not use it to identify you. We use it only to find and fix bugs and to understand which levels are too hard or too easy. Google processes it under the Google Privacy Policy and the Firebase privacy terms. Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months.

Notifications

  • Daily reminders: the Game can remind you to play at about 9 AM and 7 PM. These are created on your device; no data is sent to schedule them. You can turn them off in your phone's notification settings (Maze Brush: Paint Puzzle Game > Notifications > Daily reminders).
  • Push notifications: we may send news about new levels, events and rewards through Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google LLC). To deliver them, Firebase gives your install a random messaging token; it is not linked to your name or contact details. You can turn these off in your phone's notification settings.

On Android 13 and later the Game asks for your permission before showing any notification.

App updates

When a new version is available on Google Play, the Game can offer to update itself using Google Play's in-app update service. Google Play handles the download and installation under the Google Privacy Policy; we do not receive any extra data from it.

Permissions

  • Vibration: optional haptic feedback (can be turned off in Settings).
  • Internet / network state: to send the analytics and crash reports above and to receive push notifications.
  • Notifications: daily reminders and news (you can refuse or turn them off at any time).
  • Run at start-up: only to set the daily reminders again after your phone restarts.

Advertising (future versions)

Future versions may show ads provided by third-party ad networks. Those partners may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), app and ad-interaction data to show and measure ads. Before ads are added, this policy will be updated to list each partner, the data collected and how you can control personalised ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings at any time.

Children

The Game is suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your choices

  • Turn sound, music and vibration on or off in Settings.
  • Delete all Game data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the Game.
  • Turn daily reminders or any other notifications off in your phone's notification settings.
  • You can reset your device's app-instance and advertising IDs in your Android settings.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version.
